## How do I open a savings account?
You can open a YAH Savings account in the YAH Bank mobile app or at any branch. You need a valid Emirates ID and a UAE mobile number. The minimum opening deposit is AED 1,000.

## What is the interest rate on the savings account?
The YAH Savings account pays 3.25% per year, calculated daily and paid monthly. This rate applies to balances up to AED 500,000.

## Is there a minimum balance on current accounts?
Current accounts require a minimum average monthly balance of AED 3,000. If the balance falls below this, a fee of AED 25 per month is charged. Savings accounts have no minimum balance fee.

## How do I close my account?
You can close your account at any branch or by calling YAH Bank support on 800 0000 000. Closing is free if the account is older than 6 months. Otherwise, a closure fee of AED 100 applies.
