## How do I block a lost or stolen card?
Block your card instantly in the app under Cards > Block card, or call 800 0000 000, available 24/7. A replacement card costs AED 50 and arrives within 5 working days.

## What is the annual fee for credit cards?
The YAH Classic credit card is free for life. The YAH Platinum credit card costs AED 750 per year. The Platinum fee is waived if you spend AED 60,000 or more in a year.

## What is the foreign transaction fee?
Purchases in any currency other than AED are charged a foreign transaction fee of 2.5% of the transaction amount.

## How is the minimum credit card payment calculated?
The minimum payment is 5% of the outstanding balance or AED 100, whichever is higher. If you miss the minimum payment, a late payment fee of AED 230 is charged.
