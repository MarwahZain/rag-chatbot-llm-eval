## How much does an international transfer cost?
International transfers cost AED 25 per transfer in the mobile app and AED 50 at a branch. Transfers to GCC countries are free.

## How long does an international transfer take?
International transfers usually take 1 to 3 working days. Transfers submitted after 2:00 PM UAE time on a working day are processed on the next working day.

## What is the daily transfer limit?
The daily limit for local transfers in the app is AED 100,000. The daily limit for international transfers is AED 50,000. You can request a higher limit at any branch.

## Can I cancel a transfer?
Local transfers cannot be cancelled once sent. International transfers can be cancelled free of charge within 30 minutes of submission in the app.
